Key Topics Covered:

  • Inheritance (පරම්පරාගත බව): How classes can inherit properties from other classes.
  • Polymorphism (බහුරූපතාව): How objects can take on many forms.
  • Encapsulation (ආවරණය): Bundling data and methods together, and controlling access.
  • Abstraction (වියුක්තිය): Hiding complexity and showing only what's necessary.
  • Static Members (ස්ථිතික සාමාජිකයන්): Members that belong to the class, not to objects.
  • Pointers vs References ( указатели и ссылки): Two ways to indirectly access data.
